We'll Feed Ya and Feed Ya Good!

Daniel Boone's Grill & Tavern is unlike any restaurant you have ever visited, once you walk in, you will be looking back in history. Some, depending on your age, can re-live childhood memories with all the memorabilia that line the walls and shelves.

Although the atmosphere is something to behold, we pride ourselves in providing the best food and service for miles around. We offer delicious country cooking and down home goodness every day!

There is something for everyone on our extensive menu. From the fresh cut Idaho potatoes perfectly fried, and the hand breaded chicken fried steak, or our farm raised catfish filets, and our famous juicy burgers, we will feed ya and feed ya GOOD! To top it all off, with each meal purchase, you have free pinto beans, our famous chili beans and Blue Bell ice cream.

Mr. Daniel Boone worked night and day to build this business for what it is today. He loved this place, and he the one we all intend to make proud. We will do are best to serve you and to carry on the legacy of a great Husband, Businessman, Uncle, Grandfather, Friend and Father, not only to his 5 children, but to every employee that has graced his door.
